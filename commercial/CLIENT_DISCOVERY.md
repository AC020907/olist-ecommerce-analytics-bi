# Client Discovery Questionnaire

*Takes about 10 minutes. Short answers are fine — we will go through them together on a call.*

## 1. Your business

1. What does your company sell, and through which channels (own website, marketplaces, stores, B2B)?
2. Roughly how many orders or invoices do you process per month?
3. Who will use the dashboard (owner, sales manager, operations, finance)? How many people?

## 2. Your data

4. Where does your sales data live today? (e.g. Shopify, WooCommerce, ERP, CRM, accounting software, Excel, a database)
5. Can you export it? In which format (Excel, CSV, Google Sheets, database access, API)?
6. Which other data should be combined with sales (products/costs, customers, targets, inventory, shipping, returns, ad spend)?
7. How far back does the data go, and how far back do you want to analyse?
8. Are there known problems in the data (duplicates, missing products, manual corrections, different currencies)?

## 3. What you want to see

9. What are the 5–10 numbers you would want to see every Monday morning?
10. Which questions do you struggle to answer today?
11. How do you define revenue? (with/without tax, shipping, discounts, returns, cancellations)
12. Do you already have a report or Excel file we should reproduce or improve? Please share it.

## 4. Updates and automation

13. How often should the dashboard update? (monthly, weekly, daily)
14. Who does the reporting today, and how many hours per week does it take?
15. Do you have Power BI licences (Free/Desktop, Pro, Premium/Fabric)? Should the report be shared online?

## 5. Constraints

16. Are there technical or security constraints (no cloud, data must stay in your servers, NDA required)?
17. Should different users see different data (e.g. each sales rep only their region)?

## 6. Budget and timing

18. What budget range do you have in mind? (under US$500 / 500–1,000 / 1,000–2,500 / more)
19. Is there a deadline or event the dashboard is needed for?
20. Who will approve the work and be available to answer questions during the project?
